Gold Price Calculator

Enter the gold weight and karat to calculate an Iranian invoice with making fee, jeweler profit and VAT. The 18-karat gold price is loaded from the Tehran open market and can be changed.

Final invoice price—

Common Iranian jewelry formula: gold value = weight × price per gram adjusted for karat, making fee = a percentage of gold value, profit = a percentage of gold value plus making fee, and VAT on the making fee and profit only. For old gold, buyers usually pay only the raw gold value (and sometimes slightly less). Confirm percentages on the jeweler's invoice.

Frequently asked questions

How is a gold price with making fee calculated?

First, gold value (weight times price per gram) is calculated. The making fee is then added as a percentage of that value, the jeweler's profit as a percentage of gold plus making fee, and VAT on the making fee and profit only.

Is VAT charged on the full gold price?

No. VAT is calculated on the making fee and the jeweler's profit, not on the value of the gold itself.

How do I calculate 21- or 24-karat gold?

Choose the karat from the list. The tool adjusts the price proportionally from the 18-karat gold price.
