Karaoke Maker: Remove Vocals from a Song

Give it a song and separate vocals from the instruments: you get an instrumental karaoke version and a version with vocals only (acapella). Choose AI for quality similar to professional apps, or the fast classic method, like the vocal-removal button on karaoke devices. Nothing is uploaded; everything runs on your phone or computer, with no usage limit.

What does each part do?

  • Choose a song: Provide MP3, M4A, WAV, OGG, AAC, FLAC, or the audio from an MP4 video. The song is opened only on your device.
  • 🤖 AI: A vocal-separation model (from the MDX-Net family used in professional audio-separation software) runs on your device and identifies vocals among the instruments. It gives the best quality, works with mono songs, and keeps instruments and rhythm intact. The model file is downloaded and saved once the first time; later uses do not download it again. It may take a few minutes on a computer and longer on a phone.
  • “High quality” and “Light and fast” models: If both are available, “High quality” removes vocals more completely, while “Light and fast” is smaller and quicker. The light model removes the lead vocal and may retain backing vocals, like real karaoke tracks.
  • ⚡ Fast classic method: It does what the vocal-removal button on karaoke devices does: removes sound placed exactly in the center of the song. It is ready in seconds and downloads nothing.
  • Vocal-removal strength (fast method only): “Soft” preserves instruments better but removes less vocal; “Normal” is a good balance; “Strong” reduces the vocal and its echo as much as possible.
  • 🎤 Instrumental music: A vocal-free version for karaoke, singing practice, or use under a video.
  • 🗣️ Vocals only (acapella): The singer's voice without instruments, for remixes, learning a melody, or listening closely to lyrics.
  • Download WAV: Saves a full-quality, uncompressed output. The filename is based on your song's name.

Frequently asked questions

Which method should I choose?

Choose “AI” when quality matters. If you are in a hurry or have a slow connection, try “Fast”; it gives an acceptable result on most stereo songs.

Is my song sent to a server?

No. Both methods process everything in your browser, and the file never leaves your device. That is why there is no usage limit.

Why is AI slower the first time?

The model file and its runtime are downloaded and saved on your device once. Later uses start directly.

Does it work on phones?

Yes. The fast method is quick on all phones. The AI method works best on newer phones with Chrome; on weaker phones it may take several minutes, so keep the page open.

Why can I still hear a little vocal?

With the fast method, wide reverb or a doubled vocal cannot be removed completely; try “Strong” or AI. AI can also leave a very small remainder in dense sections.
