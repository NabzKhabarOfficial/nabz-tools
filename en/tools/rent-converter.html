Iranian Rent Deposit Converter

Enter your current rahn (rental deposit) and monthly rent to calculate the equivalent full deposit, rent with no deposit and rent with a new deposit. This is Iran's rahn/ejare (deposit-versus-monthly-rent) system; adjust the rate for your local market.

Rent with new deposit—

The common Iranian conversion rate is 3% per month: every 1 million Toman of deposit equals 30,000 Toman of monthly rent. Rates vary by city and neighborhood and are agreed by landlord and tenant. Adjust the figure to your local market.

Frequently asked questions

How is rahn converted to monthly rent?

The conversion uses a monthly rate. At the common 3% rate, every 1 million Toman of deposit equals 30,000 Toman of monthly rent. Therefore, 100 million Toman more deposit reduces monthly rent by 3 million Toman.

What does equivalent full rahn mean?

It is the deposit amount you would pay if you wanted to pay no monthly rent.
