Frequently asked questions

Which photos give the best results?

Photos with a clear subject and a simple background work best, such as an animal, car, toy, cartoon, or close-up face. For young children, “Simple” creates fewer lines and larger areas to color.

Is my photo sent to a server?

No. Conversion happens entirely in your browser, and the photo never leaves your phone or computer.

How do I print it?

Click “Print A4” to print only the drawing on one A4 sheet, or download the PNG and print it wherever you like.
