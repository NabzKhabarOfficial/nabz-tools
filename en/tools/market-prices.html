Frequently asked questions

What is today's US dollar price on the open market?

The Tehran open-market dollar price appears at the top of this page with its last update time. Rates are updated every two hours.

Why do prices change on Fridays too?

The official market is closed on Fridays; on those days, currency rates follow the live Tether price to stay closer to the real market.

Where do gold and coin prices come from?

They come from public Tehran gold and coin market sources. For a transaction, also consider a jeweler's price and the coin bubble.
