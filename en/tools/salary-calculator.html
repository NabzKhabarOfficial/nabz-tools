Frequently asked questions

What is the salary-tax exemption for 1405?

The monthly exemption is 40 million Toman (480 million Toman per year). Salary up to this threshold is not taxed.

How is 1405 salary tax calculated?

Progressively: 10% on 40–80 million Toman, 15% on 80–100 million, 20% on 100–120 million, 25% on 120–140 million and 30% above 140 million. Each part uses its own bracket rate.

What is the employee insurance rate?

The employee share of Iran's Social Security insurance is 7% of salary and benefits subject to insurance; the employer share is 23%.
