Quick help

Frequently asked questions

Are NABZ Tools free?

Yes. Every tool is free, with no sign-up and nothing to install.

Are my files and text uploaded?

No. The tools run inside your browser, so your images, PDFs and text stay on your device. Only the price tools (currency, market prices, gold) download the latest rates.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

World currency and crypto rates come from a public daily rates feed and are refreshed every two hours. The Iranian rial uses the Tehran open-market rate. Rates are indicative, not a trading quote.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. Every page is built for small screens and touch, and works in any modern mobile browser.

Is there a Persian version?

Yes. NABZ Tools started in Persian; every one of the 33 tools also has a Persian page. Use the فارسی button at the top of any page.
