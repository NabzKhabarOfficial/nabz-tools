About NABZ Tools

NABZ Tools is a free collection of small, fast online tools for everyday work: money calculators, currency rates, text and code helpers, images and PDF.

How it works

Every tool runs inside your browser. Your files and text are processed on your own device and are not uploaded to a server. There is nothing to install and no account to create.

Who makes it

NABZ Tools is made by the team behind the NABZ news channel. It started as a Persian site for users in Iran and now has an English version for everyone else.
