Privacy Policy

Short version: your files and text stay on your device.

Your data

All tools process your input inside your browser. Images, PDFs, passwords, hashes and text are not sent to our servers or to third parties.

URL Shortener (the one exception)

A link cannot be shortened without a server, so the URL Shortener sends the URL you enter to a free public shortening service (is.gd first, then v.gd, spoo.me and shrtr.top in turn if one cannot be reached), which creates the short link. Short links are public and permanent. Your list of recent links is kept only in your browser.

Exchange rates

The currency converter downloads a public rates file from this website. No personal data is sent with that request.

Statistics

We count page visits with GoatCounter, a privacy-friendly service that does not use cookies and does not track you across sites.

Local storage

Your browser remembers your theme (light or dark) and your recently used tools. This stays on your device and you can clear it at any time in your browser settings.
